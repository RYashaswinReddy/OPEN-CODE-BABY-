About

The point of HUH

We design and make our own hoodies and tees in India — real identity, honest price, no imported hype, no fake covers.

What it is

01 — Identity

HUH is a streetwear label. We own our design from sketch to stitch — heavyweight fleece, boxy drops, embroidered scripts, structured collars. Everything runs through our own local manufacturing, so the clothes carry our name and our standards, not some factory's.

The first release, Drop 1, is a deliberately small capsule — one hero hoodie and one core tee — sized to prove the model before we scale it.

The problem

02 — The gap

01

02

First copies

Cheap, low quality, no real brand behind them. You wear a logo — not a label.

03

Real cultural credibility and identity — but tiny drops, limited sizing, prices that creep up.

04

HUH sits in the gap

Real design identity + a price a normal budget can actually wear.
