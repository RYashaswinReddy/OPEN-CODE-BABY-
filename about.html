About

Real design identity, on a normal budget.

HUH designs and manufactures its own hoodies and tees in India. No importing someone else's hype, no pretending to be a bigger brand than we are — just clothes with our own identity, priced for people who actually wear them.

The problem: an awkward gap

For a 16–28 year-old in India who follows global hypebeast culture but lives on a normal budget, every option is a compromise:

Genuine imports

Real hype brands, but import duty and logistics push prices to 2–3× global retail. Out of reach for most.

First copies

Cheap, low quality, no real brand behind them. You wear a logo — not a label.

Small design labels

Real cultural credibility and real identity — but tiny drops, limited sizing, and price points that still creep up.

Funded D2C giants

Scale and accessibility, but generic designs. It fits your budget and your catalog, not your identity.

HUH sits in the gap

We're proving that a brand can have real design identity and be priced for a normal budget — between duty-inflated imports and cheap first copies.

Who it's for

  • 16–28 year-olds in India — in big cities and, increasingly, smaller ones.

  • Fans of hypebeast / streetwear culture — they follow the drops, they know the names, they can't justify the price tags.

  • People who refuse first copies — cheap knock-offs have no quality and no brand standing behind them.
