How it works

HUH pieces are unisex and cut generously. The tool turns your height and weight into a body-frame baseline, adjusts for very short or very tall frames, then applies how you like to wear it — the hoodie runs closer to your true fit, while the tee can be sized up further for that exaggerated streetwear silhouette. When you're between sizes, go up.
